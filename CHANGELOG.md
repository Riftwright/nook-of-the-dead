# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10

Requires Nookworks 1.1.0 or later (any 1.x), NeoForge 26.3. **Back up your world before updating.** The castle's roofs and
the covered market's turn are put right only where the If has not been made yet: in an If you have been in, roll a new one
to have them right.

- **1.0: out of beta.** The road from the mirror to its end, the town, the country round it and the fallen capital, as
  they are, gone over again by three reviews and a round of play.
- **The vaccine takes the capital's crows too**: a cured If burns them with the rest of the dead and keeps no flocks.
- **The thin-air zombies are undead** like the rest of the plague's creatures: Smite cuts them, a potion of healing hurts them.
- **The crows cannot be bottled** and let go outside the capital.
- **The covered market faces its street** on every lot: on about half of them it turned its back to it.
- **Books on a shelf face out** in a building that was mirrored and turned, where they faced the wall.
- **Esc skips the films** again (the opening, the ending).
- A shelter worked out for one If is never taken for another's, in a game that has rolled more than one.
- **The castle's towers wear their roofs**: the slate cones of the curtain's towers and the keep's corner turrets sat a
  block over their walls, floating; they sit on them now (where the capital is made after the update).

## 0.6.0 (beta) - 2026-10

Requires Nookworks 1.1.0 or later (any 1.x), NeoForge 26.3. **Back up your world before updating.** The seas, the
mountains and the other new ground appear only where the If has not been made yet: in an If you have been in, roll a new
one to see them.

- **Small seas** now and then in the wilds and the farmland: open water a couple of hundred blocks across, a beach round
  it, kelp and seagrass in it, a boat or two left pulled up on the sand; the tracks that crossed the land run on into it.
- **Mountains** now and then in the wilds: up from the woods to bare rock, snow at the top.
- **The capital's roofs**: about three houses in five are gabled now, the rest hipped as before.
- **The desert's edge** no longer runs along a straight line: the sand, the dry scrub and the colour of the ground wander
  in and out across it, and so do the other regions' edges.
- **Every language read through again**, all eight, and the guide with them: meanings put right where a translation had
  drifted, names made the same as the game's own, the vanilla names each language's official one.
- **The source is public** on GitHub (Riftwright/nook-of-the-dead), under the MIT licence.

## 0.5.0 (beta) - 2026-10

Requires Nookworks 1.1.0 or later (any 1.x - 1.2 included), NeoForge 26.3. **Back up your world before updating.** The
capital and the other new places appear only where the If has not been made yet: in an If you have been in, roll a new
one to see them.

- **Works with Nookworks 1.2.** 0.4 asked for a Nookworks below 1.2 and would not load beside it.
- **The Fallen Capital**, a new region: one walled city in each If, far out from the mirror.
  - Inside its moat and wall, tall houses stand wall to wall along winding lanes, their upper floors jutting over the
    lanes and meeting over the alleys; squares where the lanes meet, a market of stalls by the council's hall.
  - A river runs through it in a deep gorge: quays and cranes below, bridges across, the main one lined with shops.
  - In the middle, a crag and the castle on it, a road winding up to its gate. Inside: the great hall with its throne
    and banquet tables, the chapel, the keep's storeys - the records, the audience room, and at the top the king's own
    room, and what he kept there - and the kitchen, barracks and stables along the wall. Climb its towers and walk its
    walls.
  - The cathedral, the council's hall, an old wall's ruin, a quarter across the river that burnt; outside the gates, the
    camp of those who were never let in.
  - Every house furnished room by room - shops and workshops on the street, kitchens, living rooms, bedrooms, stores -
    with Nookworks' furniture, books and things.
  - Its own story, told by what was left: thirteen diaries and five proclamations found only there, in every language.
  - **Carrion crows** wheel in flocks round its spires and towers, and now and then one drops on someone in the open.
    They come apart from the game's spawning, so there are no fewer of the dead for them.
  - Its edge eases into the land round it: no wall of earth, no step.
- **The infected cow** in a skin of its own: rotting, its ribs bare through the torn hide, an eye gone.
- **The town's buildings** have more left in them: shelves with books and bottles, chairs at the tables, lanterns gone
  out, rugs (new ground only). The rooftop holdout on one of the town's stone houses is there only now and then;
  otherwise its roof room is a store.
- **The wilds' rocks come down to the tracks and the ruins on a slope** instead of being cut off sheer beside them
  (new ground only).
- **No rats in the refuge**: they no longer come out of the dark in it, nor round anyone in it.

## 0.4.0 (beta) - 2026-10

Requires Nookworks 1.1.0 or later (below 1.2), NeoForge 26.3. **Back up your world before updating.** The new places
in the town and the sky base's new way through appear only where you have not been yet. A refuge past the Vandal that
no one has stood in yet gets its new rooms the next time you come near it.

- **Films at the road's turns**, a few seconds each, Esc to skip: when the Vandal falls, a look to the refuge's door as
  its lamps come on and the plug draws back (for everyone near); come to the second institute, a look up the Tower from
  its foot to the base in the clouds; come up into the sky base, a look over the drop at the shut laboratory and the
  way round. Each arrival once for each player; none in creative or spectator.
- **The opening** passes a holdout's roof, its white rag, when there is one near; a bell far off, once, and somewhere a
  scream cut short.
- **The ending's credits** go back over what the town left - a shelter's well and a holdout's roof near the town.
- **Diaries are of where you find them.** The inn, the clinics, the station, the checkpoint, the farms, the tunnels,
  the sand's town and the base above the clouds each have their own first; one is found only at the top of the tower.
  Three new diaries; one rewritten.
- **New notes**: how the suppressant was made, the two who stayed at the second institute, where the keys were. The
  note in the refuge reads better.
- **The launch keys**: two at the second institute, two on the key-holders in the sky base, who always let theirs
  fall. A key on the ground never despawns and nothing destroys it; one lost all the same turns up again before the
  console. The refuge's chest has a Lode Pearl to the second institute again for whoever has none (once a day).
- A dead with a launch key in hand now drops it (it went with the rest of what the dead leave nothing of).
- **The game's difficulty counts.** Easy: smaller crowds, a bite costs two seconds, fewer of the quick, the keen, the
  wall-breakers and the screamers. Hard and Hardcore: crowds half again as big and more of them at a time, a bite costs
  twelve seconds, twice as many of the dangerous kinds, quicker to break walls, sharper ears, slow to give up a chase,
  and wounded below half health, the idle dead near you come for you. The Vandal, the thing in the sewers and the
  Sentry Golems have a quarter less health on Easy, half again as much on Hard. Normal is as it was.
- **More of the town's story** (new ground only):
  - **Holdouts on the roofs**: on the flat roofs of some buildings, what one of the living left - a bedroll, a cold fire,
    rainwater, a white rag on a pole, a diary - and sometimes the one who kept it.
  - **The church of "salvation"**: in some of the ruined churches, the pews pushed back, a ring of the ones in white
    knelt round one of the dead, white candles, and what they sang.
  - **Shelters under the town**: a dry, roofed well in the town's open ground, a ladder down to a hall the townspeople
    dug, and rooms off it - beds, stores, the sick, a place to pray, a pool, one passage fallen in.
  - **Sinkholes**: where a street fell into the galleries below, a way down by the vines.
  - **The covered market**: a timber hall of eight booths whose trades are drawn anew for each market - the baker, the
    butcher, the greengrocer, the apothecary, the smith, the tailor, the chandler, the bookseller, the potter, the cooper.
- **Three longer books** to read through: the town clerk's record book (in the squares' buildings), the town doctor's
  case notes (in the clinics), and a child's adventure book (only in the shelters under the town - and in one of them,
  what the child called treasure number one).
- **Fewer sewer blooms**: at most one in each square of about 80 blocks, and not in every square (new ground only).
- **Rats again**: now and then a pack of them comes with the dead in the town, or out of the dark down in the galleries.
- **Infected Cats** in the zombie villages: in the houses, and now and then among the dead that come near a village.
  Small and quick, their bite counts against the infection, and they leap - onto fences, walls and low roofs the dead
  cannot get onto (not up a ladder). More of them on Hard, fewer on Easy.
- **The refuge, lived in.** Its door in the Vandal's hall is marked now, and sealed with a plug nothing breaks: it opens when the Vandal dies, and not before. A Vandal gone without dying is put back in its hall (never two), and Peaceful no longer takes it away. Inside: a
  decontamination room, a kitchen, cubicles in the dormitory, and a lower floor down a stair from the machine hall -
  the machine hall two storeys high with more machines, the records room, the chief's room, an isolation room, a cold
  room, a washroom. Three diaries are found only there. A refuge nobody has stood in yet gets all this the first time
  you come near it.
- **The sky base, a dungeon.** The laboratory is shut off from the hub and stands out over the drop; the way in is
  round and up - the quarters' stair, a landing on their roof, a catwalk in the wind, a bridge, the laboratory's roof
  and its gallery. Off the way: the director's room, the radio mast, the water tank, an infirmary, a washroom, a cold
  store. Only where the base has not been made yet.
- **The guide**, in all its languages: what to aim for, the road from the first map scrap to the end set out step by
  step (folded shut with the other spoilers), the town's new places with pictures, and no more counting of diaries.

## 0.3.0 (beta) - 2026-10

Requires Nookworks 1.1.0 or later (below 1.2), NeoForge 26.3. **Back up your world before updating.** The If's sky
grows taller (only empty space is added above). Places you have already been get none of what is new; it appears
where you have not been yet - except the refuge past the Vandal, which gets what it should hold the next time you go
in. If what lies further down the road falls where you have been, start the If again.

- **An end to it.** Past the Vandal the road goes on - to a second institute far off, up a tower into the sky, to a
  base above the clouds where the dead and its old guards wait. At the end of it, a way to cure the If, if you find the
  four keys it wants. While it runs, the dead are gone from it, and only the ordinary monsters come out at night, as in
  the overworld; stop it, and they come back. The ruins stay.
- **A taller If**, up to height 447, and above 352 a sky of its own.
- **The first time over there**, a short film; the dead hold back and pay you no mind till it ends. Esc skips it.
- **Suppressant** lasts fifteen minutes now, the dizziness three minutes before the end. Redstone on the brewing stand
  makes a long one, thirty minutes. Chests hold both, and now and then a splash one.
- **The Vandal** is fought hand to hand: arrows, tridents and thrown things do nothing to it.
- **Loopholes shut** (with Nookworks): the Vandal is a boss and the plague's creatures cannot be bottled, so no
  spawner of them; dying at home no longer ends the infection - it waits where it stood; the Vandal comes for the
  nearest player when a laser hits it from out of its sight. A floor of glass or slabs no longer keeps the dead off:
  they come out on glass and bottom slabs, and the horde's runners on stairs too.
- **What lies past the Vandal** is reached only through its hall: the rock round it does not give.
- **On Hard and in Hardcore**, a roofed, lit room no longer keeps the newly come from being sent to you; Dark Matter's
  reach and the refuge still do.
- **Diaries**: thirty-six of them, some only of everyday life. None comes twice until you have read them all.
- **Underground**: the old galleries have more of the people who used them - barrels, bedrolls in niches, rubble,
  tables - and now and then a door in the wall with an empty hideout behind it.
- **In the sewers**, something grew. It does not move. Do not stand where it can reach.
- **Crates** in the buildings now and then hold something too.
- **Nookworks 1.1's things in the ruins.** Books lie about the town as they were left - on desks, stood in rows against
  a Bookend on the shelves, fallen to the floor - most of them plain, now and then a diary or an enchanted one. The
  research institutes are thick with them, and notes their people wrote are among them; other places have notes of
  their own. Barricades, walls and hideouts are patched with rusting Corrugated Sheets, Scrap Bales and Patched
  Sheet Metal; where people held out to the last, the windows are sheeted over.
- **New places**: a scrapyard, the town's library gone to ruin, a camp walled in sheet.
- **The refuge** past the Vandal has a workshop of Nookworks' machines on its own line, and the base above the clouds
  another.
- The guide, in all its languages, for all of the above (what lies at the end folded shut).

## 0.2.1 (beta) - 2026-10

Requires Nookworks 1.0.0 or later (below 1.1), NeoForge 26.3. Back up your world before updating. Institutes you
have already found keep the depths they were made with; what lies past the Vandal is in those not yet generated.

- **Room to breathe.** Fewer of the dead by day (40 round you in the town, not 55; the night is as it was). Each one
  you put down keeps one more away for a minute and a half, so a street you cleared stays clear a while.
- **Shelter that shelters.** In a roofed, lit room, in Dark Matter's reach or in the refuge, the newly come are no
  longer sent to you - only what hears or sees you comes - and within Dark Matter's reach none break their way in.
- **Sleep behind walls.** The dead that cannot see you no longer keep you awake.
- **Past the Vandal.** Something was sealed off beyond its hall. Worth the trip.
- **The Vandal** must be fought: nothing of the place hurts it - lava, fire, drowning, Liquid Experience, mercury -
  only what a player does to it (Nookworks' laser included), and nothing from inside the refuge. Its hall keeps its
  pillars now. The dead and the Vandal no longer break doors on Hard.
- **Suppressant** is rarer in what was left lying about (a store-house no longer holds a dozen doses); where medicine
  was kept, as before.
- Iron Pots sit on their Grills over a fire, as Nookworks has them; bodies lie clear of what stands by them; the
  institute and two of the town's buildings no longer hold their furnishings twice over (forty iron armour stands
  stood in one spot in the institute's depths).
- Advancements: Till Morning comes when you wake to the morning, Bottled Hope only for a suppressant you brewed.
- The guide, in all its languages: the shelters as they now work, a Lode Pearl to get home, the crowd's numbers, the
  named dead, the Vandal. Its advancement is renamed.

## 0.2.0 (beta) - 2026-10

Everything is in. Numbers and rules may still change, and a world may not carry over to later versions unchanged.

Requires Nookworks 1.0.0 or later (below 1.1), NeoForge 26.3.

- **The desert.** Part of the wilds and farmland is now a town the sand took: dunes over the streets, buildings worn to
  their footings, sandstorms in place of rain, dried fields, camps and shafts the players left. Pyramids still stand -
  some untouched, some already dug out. The desert's own buildings: a caravanserai, a cistern house, a camel stable,
  courtyard houses, a covered bazaar, an archaeologists' dig whose suspicious sand can still be brushed, and, rarely, a
  great timber workshop or inn.
- **The desert's dead.** Mutated husks (their blow leaves you hungry), infected camels that ram, infected scorpions
  whose sting poisons.
- **Bells.** Ring one and every one of the dead near it comes. To call them away, or down on yourself.
- **Screamers.** Now and then one of the dead screams when it sees you, and the others come.
- **Weather.** Rain and storms close the world in.
- **Traces of what happened.** Carts left in the streets, barricades, walled-off blocks, burial grounds, canals with
  their boats, buildings fallen in or grown through by trees, the bands where fire went through.
- **The makeshift grenade.** Thrown, it bursts after a moment and puts down the dead round it; no block is broken. It
  is loud.
- **More buildings.** A field hospital, a market hall held from its roof, a prison made a settlement, a bell tower, a
  morgue, a school with a roof garden, a water tower, a checkpoint.
- **The Vandal** has a new look - and a face to fear.
- Smite works on all the plague's creatures (they are undead now); Bane of Arthropods on the scorpion. Looting works.
- Zombie villages sit level with the ground, out in the country only.
- Streets bend and follow the ground; worn paths cross the open ground.
- Spanish, Russian and Brazilian Portuguese, as Nookworks has them (the Spanish also for Latin America, the Brazilian
  also for Portugal).
- `/nzw start strong` (cheats on): start the If armed to the teeth, for trying it out.
- Many fixes: the Vandal's size, its fall and minecarts; houses facing their streets; nothing left floating.

**Worlds from 0.1.0:** places you have not been yet generate with the new content; the desert can appear next to land
you have already explored, with a hard edge between them.

## 0.1.0 (alpha) - 2026-10

The first public build: everything is in, but it has not been played through yet. Expect rough edges; worlds made with
an alpha may not carry over to later versions.

Requires Nookworks 1.0.0 or later (below 1.1), NeoForge 26.3.

- **The fallen If.** Nookworks' If becomes a world whose civilisation of players fell to the dead: the town (centre, old
  town, residential, outskirts, waste), the players' settlements, overgrown farmland with fields of every kind, the
  wilds with crags, woods, thickets, caves and small lakes; zombie villages and ruined portals now and then. Under the
  ground, old mine galleries and, under the town, its sewers - rooms people lived in, bunkers, rats' nests, barricades
  and traps. Over fifty buildings (a hospital and a railway station among them), each lot's in its own timber, colours
  and state of ruin, some boarded up; survivors' remains here and there. Each region its own air: fog, silence, distant
  sounds. The If Mirror shows it.
- **Mutated zombies.** Fast, sun-proof, each a little different (pace, flanking, senses, how they break through);
  they see only what is in sight and hear the rest - sneak, or walk on carpet, and walls keep you unheard; they break
  wood, glass and earth, not stone or iron; they cannot climb ladders. They drop nothing. Mutated zombie villagers,
  zombie cows, horses and wolves, infected bats and rats; zoglins in the farmland.
- **The infection.** You are infected the moment you step in. Suppressants, found in the ruins (and brewed once you
  have what the Vandal leaves), hold it back; splash one on a mutated zombie and it is an ordinary zombie again. A
  bitten friend gets up as one of them.
- **The Vandal.** A research institute stands somewhere; the scraps of a map lead to it, and at its bottom waits the
  boss.
- **Shelter.** Under a roof and lit, none come; nor within reach of Nookworks' Dark Matter. Beds work; death takes you
  home.
- **No farming the dead.** They take no boats or minecarts; a name tag, a lead or a pit does not keep them; what dies
  in a trap leaves nothing.
- Diaries left in the ruins, twelve of them.
- An advancement tab; English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) and Korean.
- Data packs can add buildings to the town (`data/<namespace>/nookofthedead/buildings/*.json`).
